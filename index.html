About Us

Who are we?

Technominds is the premier IT club of the Department of CS & Applications , established to foster a culture of technological innovation and collaborative learning among students.

The mission is to bridge the gap between academic knowledge and industry requirements by organizing workshops, hackathons, seminars, and tech talks that equip students with practical skills.

Innovation

We encourage creative thinking and innovative solutions to real-world problems.

Collaboration

We believe in the power of teamwork and collaborative projects.

Development

We focus on enhancing coding skills and software development practices.

Learning

We provide a platform for continuous learning and skill development.

Message from the Teacher Incharge

Dr. Harmunish Taneja

Innovation and creativity are the foundations on which the edifice of knowledge truly thrives. TechnoMinds: The IT club, is a reflection of this spirit—where youthful curiosity meets purposeful learning. The club has not only become a platform for technical exploration but also a space where ideas are nurtured into meaningful solutions.

At the heart of TechnoMinds lies the zeal of our students. Their energy, dedication, and willingness to learn is the driving force behind every initiative; be it technical workshops, hackathons, or interactive sessions with experts from IT domain. These efforts are the milestones in the journey of bridging academic learning with real-world problem solving. The success of the club is due to the students who bring freshness, creativity, and commitment to everything they do. The youthful spirit strengthens the legacy of TechnoMinds and ensures that the club continues to grow as a hub of collaborative learning, innovation, and excellence.

With best wishes for your continued growth,
